## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Characterized artifact responsibility extraction
The system SHALL preserve observable fabrication and verification contracts during scoped responsibility extraction.

#### Scenario: Runtime distributions
- **WHEN** locked runtime distributions are prepared through the real vendor builder
- **THEN** bundles, copied assets, licenses and ordered manifest retain their characterized bytes and behavior
- **AND** preliminary refusals retain their error and mutation ordering

#### Scenario: Public assembly and verification
- **WHEN** a site is assembled or its archive is verified
- **THEN** publication filtering, identity, image references and integrity refusals retain their characterized behavior
- **AND** sources and extracted functions remain measured and protected

#### Scenario: Truthful quality evidence
- **WHEN** results are compared or delivery is reported
- **THEN** baseline, scanners, scope, tested SHA and artifact evidence are identified explicitly
- **AND** a successful pull request is not presented as a merged or published delivery
